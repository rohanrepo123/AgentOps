# INC-017: Duplicate payment after timeout

## Summary
A production incident affected `svc-payment`.

## Root Cause
The charge was retried after a timeout without an idempotency key.

## Resolution
Use idempotency keys for every charge request.

## Investigation Notes
Correlate logs, service metrics, deployment history, feature flags, database state and the
service-specific runbook. Historical incidents are supporting evidence, not definitive proof.
