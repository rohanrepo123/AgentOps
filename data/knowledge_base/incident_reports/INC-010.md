# INC-010: Rate Limiting Anomaly

## Summary
Valid clients received unexpected 429 responses.

## Root Cause
Distributed rate-limit counters were inconsistent during burst traffic.

## Resolution
Improve counter consistency, reduce hot-key contention, and add 429 anomaly monitoring.
