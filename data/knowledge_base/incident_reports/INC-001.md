# INC-001: Duplicate Charges After Subscription Upgrade

## Summary
Customers were charged twice after an upstream payment timeout.

## Timeline
10:31:02 payment request started
10:31:05 provider timeout
10:31:05 automatic retry
10:31:07 charge succeeded
10:31:08 second charge succeeded

## Root Cause
A non-idempotent charge operation was automatically retried after an ambiguous upstream timeout.

## Evidence
- transaction `txn-100500`
- payment attempts `att-00500-1` through `att-00500-3`
- payment log entries
- elevated retry rate
- payment retry feature flag enabled
- payment documentation explicitly forbids unsafe retries

## Resolution
Disable unsafe charge retries and require idempotency keys.

## Lessons
A timeout does not prove that the provider did not process the request.
