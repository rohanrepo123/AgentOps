# INC-004: Delayed Email Notifications

## Summary
Invoice and password-reset emails were delayed.

## Root Cause
The background worker queue became saturated during increased traffic.

## Evidence
- queue depth increased from normal levels to roughly 4,800
- worker timeout errors
- notification provider remained reachable

## Resolution
Increase worker concurrency and configure queue backpressure.
