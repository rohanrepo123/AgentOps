# INC-022: Invoice pending surge

## Summary
A production incident affected `svc-invoice`.

## Root Cause
Invoice jobs accumulated because downstream payment data was delayed.

## Resolution
Add dependency health checks and bounded job retries.

## Investigation Notes
Correlate logs, service metrics, deployment history, feature flags, database state and the
service-specific runbook. Historical incidents are supporting evidence, not definitive proof.
