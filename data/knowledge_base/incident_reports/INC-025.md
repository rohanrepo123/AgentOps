# INC-025: Webhook delivery retries

## Summary
A production incident affected `svc-webhook`.

## Root Cause
Transient provider failures triggered bounded retries and temporary queue growth.

## Resolution
Tune retry backoff and alert only on sustained backlog.

## Investigation Notes
Correlate logs, service metrics, deployment history, feature flags, database state and the
service-specific runbook. Historical incidents are supporting evidence, not definitive proof.
